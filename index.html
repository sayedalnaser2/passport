🛂 Passport Reader
🔒 100% on your device

Bahraini passport reader

Drop many passport scans (JPG, PNG or PDF). The machine-readable zone is read, check-digits are verified, and everything lands in one sheet you can edit and download as Excel / CSV.


  
Tip: use a straight, well-lit photo/scan where the two lines at the bottom of the photo page (P<BHR…) are visible. Besides the MRZ, the printed fields (place of birth, issue date, authority…) are read and compared. ✔ = verified by check digits and printed page, ≈ = MRZ ok but printed page differs, ⚠ = needs checking. Click a row number for the debug log; correct the MRZ lines (last two columns) and the fields re-calculate. The OCR engine (~10 MB) downloads once from a CDN; the passport images never leave your device.
